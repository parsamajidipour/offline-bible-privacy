Privacy Policy

Offline Bible: Read & Study · Effective 3 November 2026

Golden Vision built Offline Bible: Read & Study as an ad-supported Bible reader. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Core app data

The Bible text is bundled with the app and core reading works offline. The app does not require an account. Bookmarks, reading position, theme, text size, and translation preferences are stored locally on your device. Golden Vision does not receive this local reading data.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device or other identifiers, IP address, general location derived from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, secure, measure, and—where permitted by your choices—personalise ads.

Where required, the app asks for your advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. You can review those choices from the app's settings when the privacy-options entry point is required.

Sharing and retention

Golden Vision does not sell your personal information. Advertising data is processed by Google and its partners under their own retention and security practices. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's information about partner sites and apps.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. You can also reset or manage your device's advertising identifier through Android settings. Where available, use the advertising privacy choices in the app's settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when we do. For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.
